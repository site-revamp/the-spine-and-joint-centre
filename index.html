A considered approach to care

Your condition is individual. Your care should be too.

At The Spine & Joint Centre, chiropractic care is built around understanding the individual, their symptoms and the way their condition affects everyday life.

The clinic has served patients in North West London since 2001. Its chiropractors work with people experiencing spinal and joint problems, headaches, sports and work-related injuries, and posture-related concerns.

The focus is on assessing your particular needs, explaining the findings and discussing an appropriate approach to care.

Understanding your needs

What brings people to chiropractic care?

From spinal discomfort to sports-related injuries, the clinic works with patients experiencing a range of musculoskeletal concerns.

Back & spinal pain

Assessment and chiropractic care for people experiencing lower back pain, spinal discomfort and related problems.

Neck & shoulder pain

Individual assessment for neck discomfort, shoulder pain and associated movement restrictions.

Headaches & migraines

Assessment of headache and migraine-related concerns as part of an individual's wider clinical picture.

Sports injuries

Chiropractic assessment for people experiencing injuries and physical problems associated with sport.

Posture-related concerns

Understanding how posture, daily activities and movement patterns may relate to your symptoms.

Joint pain & sciatica

Assessment of joint problems, disc-related concerns and sciatica within an individual treatment plan.
