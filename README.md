# The Spine & Joint Centre – Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

The Spine & Joint Centre, formerly known as Richdales Chiropractic Centre, is an established chiropractic clinic in Perivale, Greenford, providing chiropractic and musculoskeletal care since 2001.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the clinic's experience, personalised approach to treatment and chiropractic expertise. It targets individuals experiencing back pain, neck pain, joint problems, sports injuries and other musculoskeletal conditions.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Messaging and Positioning
Replaced generic and repetitive messaging with clearer communication of the clinic's chiropractic expertise, experience since 2001 and personalised approach to patient care. The revised content establishes the clinic's identity and helps visitors understand its professional offering from the outset.

### 2. Better Organisation of Services and Patient Information
Improved the presentation of conditions treated, chiropractic services, practitioner information, consultation procedures and treatment fees. This addresses the fragmented information on the previous website and makes essential patient information easier to find and understand.

### 3. Stronger Trust Signals and Booking Journey
Improved the visibility and integration of practitioner credentials, patient testimonials, transparent treatment pricing and online appointment booking. This creates a clearer journey from discovering the clinic and evaluating its services to making an appointment.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual Hierarchy:** Strengthened the presentation of the clinic's positioning, treatment expertise and primary booking opportunities.
- **Content Layout:** Organised services, conditions treated, practitioner details and patient information into clearer homepage sections.
- **Trust and Credibility:** Gave greater prominence to professional credentials, patient feedback and published treatment fees.
- **User Experience:** Made essential information easier to discover and connected relevant content with appointment booking opportunities.
- **Design Consistency:** Improved consistency across typography, spacing, section structure and visual presentation.
- **Conversion Focus:** Created a more direct path towards online appointment booking and telephone enquiries.

## Project Objective

The redesigned homepage provides a clearer representation of The Spine & Joint Centre's chiropractic expertise and established presence. It brings essential service, practitioner and pricing information together while creating a more confident and straightforward experience for prospective patients.
